### Explaining How I Would Answer My Research Question

**Question: Do cars with larger engines generally get worse gas mileage?**

To answer this question, I would use the Cars dataset and compare the engine sizes of different vehicles with their fuel efficiency. I would look at the engine size and miles per gallon (MPG) columns in the dataset. I would separate the cars into groups based on their engine sizes and calculate the average MPG for each group. Then, I would compare the results to see whether cars with larger engines generally have lower MPG than cars with smaller engines. I could also create a graph to show the relationship between engine size and fuel efficiency. This would help me understand how engine size relates to gas mileage.
