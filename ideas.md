# CS130 Semester Project Ideas

## Dataset 1: Cars

**Dataset Link:** https://corgis-edu.github.io/corgis/csv/cars/

I chose this dataset because I am interested in cars and how different vehicle specifications affect performance.

**Questions:**
- How does horsepower affect a car's fuel efficiency?
- Do cars with larger engines generally get worse gas mileage?
- Which car manufacturers have the best average fuel efficiency?

## Dataset 2: Video Games

**Dataset Link:** https://corgis-edu.github.io/corgis/csv/video_games/

I chose this dataset because I think it would be interesting to compare different video games and their ratings.

**Questions:**
- Which video game genres have the highest average ratings?
- Do more popular video games tend to have higher ratings?
- How do ratings differ between gaming platforms?
